You don’t need a huge bench to fix and build things. A handful of reliable basics covers most jobs, and the rest can come when a project actually needs it.

## Topics in this note

- Soldering iron with adjustable temperature
- Multimeter
- Wire strippers and flush cutters
- Crimping tool
- Heat shrink and a heat source
- Screwdrivers, tweezers, and good light

Buy the basics you’ll use every week first. Specialized tools can wait until a project calls for them.

## What I learned

Good light and a magnifier matter almost as much as the tools themselves.

## What I’d do differently

I’d organize parts and tools from the start instead of after the bench is already a mess.
