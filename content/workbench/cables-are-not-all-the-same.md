Two cables can look identical and behave completely differently. Some of that matters a lot, like thin wire on a power lead, and some of it is mostly marketing.

## Topics in this note

- Wire gauge and why it matters for power and long runs
- Shielding, and where noise actually comes from
- Connector quality and strain relief
- Charge-only USB cables vs. data cables
- Balanced vs. unbalanced audio connections
- When an expensive cable is worth it, and when it isn’t

A cheap cable isn’t automatically bad, but a cable that’s failing usually fails at the connector.

## What I learned

Most cable problems are at the ends: worn connectors, broken strain relief, or a loose shield.

## What I’d do differently

I’d label cables by what they can actually do (power only, data, length) instead of keeping a mystery drawer.

## Tools & Parts (examples)

- Continuity tester or multimeter
- Labels or tape and a marker
- Spare connectors
- Heat shrink
