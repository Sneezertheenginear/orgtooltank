The most useful projects are often small: a button that does one thing, a sensor that reports one number, a display that shows one status. Keeping the scope small is what gets them finished.

## Topics in this note

- Starting with one clear job
- Choosing sensors and buttons
- Powering a project safely
- Small displays and status lights
- Enclosures that hold up to daily use
- Testing on a breadboard before soldering

Finished and simple beats ambitious and half-built.

## What I learned

Power and the enclosure take more planning than the circuit itself.

## What I’d do differently

I’d choose the enclosure first, then fit the parts to it.

## Tools & Parts (examples)

- Breadboard and jumper wires
- Microcontroller board
- Sensors and buttons
- Small display
- Project enclosure
- Power supply or battery holder
