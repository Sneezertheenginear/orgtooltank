A lot of “dead” electronics are really a bad connection. The fault comes and goes, it changes when you wiggle something, and it’s easy to blame the wrong part.

## Topics in this note

- Wiggle testing, carefully
- Checking continuity end to end
- Cleaning oxidized or dirty contacts
- Spotting loose or badly done crimps
- Cracked solder joints on board-mounted jacks
- When to replace the connector instead of fixing it

If a problem moves when you move the cable, start at the connector.

## What I learned

Intermittent faults are usually mechanical. Find what changes the fault, then look closely there.

## What I’d do differently

I’d test the cable on its own before taking the device apart.

## Tools & Parts (examples)

- Multimeter
- Contact cleaner
- Crimping tool (matched to the terminals)
- Soldering iron and solder
- Magnifier or loupe
